**Figure 1 | Individual rates of adolescent cortical thinning track genetic risk and symptoms of adolescent-onset mental illness.**
CT and ΔCT are the intercept and slope of one linear mixed model per region (a) or on the per-scan cortex-wide mean (b–h): CT is thickness at the sample-mean scan age (12.8 years), not at the first scan, and ΔCT is the per-year change (negative = thinning). Both are fitted from all of a child's scans.

- **a** Group maps per parcel (bilateral mean, left hemisphere shown): mean per-child CT (1.7–3.8 mm) and ΔCT (-33 to -0.1 µm / year).
- **b** Age at scan by visit; 8,716 children, 26,946 scans; 2 / 3 / 4 scans per child for 2,205 / 3,508 / 3,003 children.
- **c** Per-scan cortical mean vs age for 250 random children (grey), three children offset from the fit line (colour; two with four scans, one with three), OLS line of best fit -19 µm / year (red). Title: the linear mixed model fitted to each child's scans, whose child-level intercept and slope are CT and ΔCT.
- **d** ΔCT reliability 1 − v/τ², where v is a child's conditional (posterior) variance of the slope random effect and τ² the between-child slope variance: boxes, per-parcel LMMs (358 parcels; each value the mean over children; medians 0.15 / 0.21 / 0.25 for 2 / 3 / 4 scans); squares, the single LMM on the cortical mean (mean over children 0.17 / 0.32 / 0.38).
- **e** Whole-cortex CT and ΔCT for each of the 8,716 children (single LMM on the per-scan cortical mean; CT at the sample-mean scan age, 12.8 years): scatter, with marginal densities (CT blue, ΔCT red). Per-child estimates are shrunk toward the mean (ΔCT SD 2.3 µm/yr against a model between-child SD of 4.2 µm/yr); every child's estimated ΔCT is negative, and CT and ΔCT are nearly independent (r = 0.10).
- **f** PRS-CS polygenic score association with ΔCT (red) and CT (blue) on each row; β per SD of score with 95% CI; filled = p < 0.05. Schizophrenia (2025 GWAS): ΔCT p = 0.01 EUR / 0.004 pooled, CT p = 0.30 / 0.81. C+T and SBayesRC give the same ΔCT pattern (SI). * p < 0.05, ** p < 0.01, *** p < 0.001 (uncorrected).
- **g** Symptoms against the same single-LMM phenotypes (ΔCT red, CT blue; each fitted alone; filled = p < 0.05; * p < 0.05, ** p < 0.01, *** p < 0.001). CBCL (parent report, log1p raw scale sums): symptoms at ages ~15–17 (mean of the available waves 5–7) adjusted for the same score at baseline (ANCOVA; not a difference score and not a per-child symptom slope), y_late ~ phenotype + y_baseline + age_late + sex + site, β in SD of y_late. KSADS: diagnosis (present or past) at any administered session from baseline to year 6, i.e. lifetime and including baseline cases, logistic with age at the last CBCL, odds ratio per SD. Both phenotypes are signed as in e–f (ΔCT negative = faster thinning), so β < 0 or OR < 1 means more symptoms with faster thinning or with thinner cortex. Estimates are unchanged when both phenotypes enter one model (CT–ΔCT r = 0.06). Family-clustered SEs; n = 8,189 (CBCL change) and 8,716 (KSADS). Faster thinning goes with rising depressive (p = 0.003), externalising (p = 0.03) and general (p-factor, p = 0.05) symptoms and parent-reported MDD (OR 0.88, p = 0.001); thinner cortex (CT) goes with youth-reported MDD (OR 0.93, p = 0.02) and parent-reported psychosis spectrum (OR 0.89, p = 0.02). Exploratory, uncorrected.

Methods
- ABCD release 7.0, HCP-MMP1.0 (358 parcels; 1 lh polygon without a value drawn grey); 8,716 children with >= 2 QC-passing scans.
- Slope model: thickness ~ age + sex + (1 + age | child) + (1 | site), per parcel (a, d) and on the per-scan cortical mean (c, e–g); the trait is the child's age slope.
- PRS: PRS-CS shown (C+T and SBayesRC in SI); EUR arm scored with European discovery GWAS, pooled arm with multi-ancestry GWAS, z-scored within ancestry cluster; model score + age + sex + 10 PCs + (1 | family); filled = p < 0.05.
- MAGMA gene-set tests (SCZ/MDD common-variant and exome sets, three LD references) are in Supplementary Information: no set is enriched for ΔCT once ABCD's own EUR children are the LD reference.
- Sensitivity analyses (DK parcellation, mean of per-parcel slopes, PGC3 2022 GWAS, SBayesR, per-ancestry strata) in Supplementary Information.
