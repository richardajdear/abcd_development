Slope components across atlases

Region loadings of PC1–PC3 from a PCA of subject-by-region thinning slopes, fitted separately on the Desikan–Killiany (DK, 68 regions) and HCP-MMP (358 parcels, hippocampal parcel excluded) parcellations of the same 8,716 ABCD children, 7.0 tabulation. Compared with each atlas's regional absolute thinning rate and with the AHBA transcriptomic components C1–C3.

Structural-covariance PCs are these same components. Eigenvectors of the region-by-region correlation matrix of subject slopes (68×68 DK, 358×358 HCP) correlate 1.000 with the PC1–PC3 loadings shown here on both atlases, because PCA on the standardised subject-by-region matrix diagonalises R while PCA on R diagonalises R² — same eigenvectors, squared eigenvalues. Every panel below is therefore also the structural-covariance result. Only the variance-explained convention differs: the squared-eigenvalue share would quote PC1 at 80% (DK) and 79% (HCP); the true variance share is the one given here.
